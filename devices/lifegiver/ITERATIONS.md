# Lifegiver — growing the interaction idea

_2026-10-15. Each step builds on the one before._

1. Start: listen → ghost on a function field → drag puck → dials glide → tweak dials.
2. Problem: two layers to manage (field + dials). What single object could hold both "what it is" and "where it goes"?
3. Producers already picture a mix as a stage of objects: height = frequency, front/back = distance, size = mass (Gibson's mix picture). Use that.
4. The reading draws the sound as an object (a blob) on that stage. Side view: x = back ↔ front, y = low ↔ high.
5. It holds up in theory: pitch–height is a robust association; front/back follows distance cues (level, top loss, transient sharpness, direct/room ratio); size follows mass.
6. The ghost blob is the sound as read. You reshape a copy; the difference between ghost and copy is the processing.
7. The reading's uncertainty becomes how fuzzy the ghost's edges are. No extra indicator.
8. Gestures: drag the body = distance. Top edge = Bright (up adds, down trims). Bottom edge = Thick.
9. Rhythm (carry ↔ drive) maps onto the blob's edges in depth: the edge facing you = attack (sharp ↔ round = Punch); the trailing edge = tail length (Sustain). A harder attack also reads as closer, so the metaphor stays consistent.
10. Moving forward or back changes several things at once (presence, attack, steadiness, top, ambience). That is the "matrix": one gesture, many means, chosen by the reading.
11. Width can't show in a side view, so it stays a small knob.
12. Texture and Steady can't be drawn cleanly either, so they stay small knobs.
13. Space is no longer a knob: distance sets it (further back = a touch more room), with an offset on the precision page.
14. Amount stays: one knob for less or more of everything.
15. Faint role names on the stage (Bed at the back, Lead at the front). Clicking one reshapes the copy to that role's template for this sound, as a fast start.
16. Check against trap A: the edges set targets, not EQ bands. The device picks the means (shelf, dynamic band, harmonics) per sound and position.
17. Check against trap B: the whole device is one picture you can grab.
18. Layout fits Live's 169 px: stage ~160 px square on the left, four small knobs on the right (Texture, Steady, Wide, Amount), "…" for the precision page.
19. Future payoff: other Lifegiver instances could show their blobs faintly on the same stage, giving a picture of the whole mix and where sounds collide. The metaphor scales to the Mix layer without a redesign.

## Where it landed
The sound is a shape on a stage. Listen draws it; you grab and reshape it; the device works out how.
